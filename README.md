## Profile

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). His PhD supervisor is Professor Junxian He. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Research Interests & Skills

- Natural language processing
- Machine learning
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability
